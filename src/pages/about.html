The Story Behind Craftsflow

How We Solved The $5,000 Placement Fee Problem

We originally started as a technical software and web development company. We engineered high-performance websites, custom platforms, and digital systems for a wide variety of clients, including senior care facilities, assisted living centers, and home care providers.

"One day, one of our assisted living clients called me directly and said: 'Arman, can you help us get clients directly? We're currently forced to rely on placement agencies that charge us $4,000 to $5,000 for every single placement! It's destroying our profit margins.'"

Hearing this, we immediately went to work. We conducted deep research into how healthcare referrals actually happen. We realized that senior care facilities didn't need to pay middleman placement agencies $5k per resident: they just needed a direct connection to local hospital discharge planners, case managers, senior nurses, and local physicians.

We built a complete multi-platform digital presence for the client, backed by a specialized LinkedIn B2B Connection & Outreach Engine designed specifically to reach healthcare decision-makers in their local zip codes. We managed their content, social media, and direct messaging on their behalf.

It took 4 to 5 months to build relationships and mature the pipeline, but once it started flowing, they received a consistent stream of direct patient referrals every single month. Instead of paying $5,000 for every placement, they now pay us a tiny fixed monthly fee to keep their growth engine running smoothly.

Today, Craftsflow takes this exact direct-relationship model and applies it to senior care, healthcare clinics, home services, and local businesses nationwide.
