$32,000 in Service Jobs in 30 Days

How database reactivation campaigns and automated SMS sequencers reactivated cold contacts into booked service calls.

Client Sarah P., Apex Plumbing & HVAC
Location Austin, TX Metro
Services Used Database Reactivation, Automated Follow-Up
Investment Return 11x ROI
38 Booked Calls & $32,000 Revenue

By launching a focused SMS check-in campaign on her customer database, Sarah secured 38 high-value service appointments from contacts who hadn't booked in over a year.

The Challenge

Sarah had a database of over 450 past clients she had serviced for one-off repairs. She had no automated system to check in, run seasonal HVAC tune-ups, or follow up, leaving significant repeat business on the table.

Our Custom Strategy

We loaded her list into our system and launched a seasonal winback campaign:

  • SMS Value Loop: Sent a text asking: "Hey [Name], we're running a seasonal check-in. Has your system been tuned up for the summer, or did you want us to handle that?"
  • Automatic Follow-Up: For leads who replied, we instantly triggered availability options.
  • Calendar Bookings: Linked hot replies straight to her dispatch calendar widget.
