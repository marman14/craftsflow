The Challenge

The clinic was buying shared leads from general medical directory portals. Each inquiry was shared with multiple clinics, leading to a race on price and low conversion. Their staff was losing hours chasing cold inquiries, and after-hours web submissions were ignored until the next business day.

Our Custom Strategy

We implemented a three-tier localized growth system:

  • Exclusive Local Leads: Target Facebook and Google ads to zip codes within a 10-mile radius, offering a low-friction initial assessment.
  • 60-Second Recovery: Triggered automatic SMS responses to every new inquiry, web form fill, or missed call, qualifying interests instantly.
  • Automated AI Booking: Our conversational system handled calendar booking 24/7 without needing staff interaction.

The Results & Verification

The metrics speak for themselves:

  • 342 exclusive health leads captured.
  • 114 patient appointments booked.
  • 87% show-up rate on scheduled bookings.
  • $45,000+ in immediate treatment plan revenue.
