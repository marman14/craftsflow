THE TIMELINE MAP

How The Tracks Fit Together

These four tracks don't run in isolation — the order matters:

MONTH 1 FOUNDATION

Track 1 (Website) and Track 4, Steps 1–2 (full digital build-out, ongoing content) run in parallel. Your presence has to be solid everywhere before anyone starts pointing referral sources or paid traffic back to it.

MONTH 1–2 INTEGRATION

Track 3 (CRM & AI Assistant) gets configured alongside the build, so missed calls and inquiries are captured from day one instead of after everything else launches.

MONTH 2 LAUNCH & OUTREACH

Track 2 (Paid Ads) goes live once Google verification clears and your profile and reviews are in place. Track 4, Steps 3–4 (referral outreach) begin now too — there's finally something credible for a case manager to find when they look you up.

ONGOING SCALING & NURTURE

Track 2 optimization, Track 3 automation, and Track 4 referral nurture all run continuously in the background to maintain full occupancy.

TRACK 1

Website Development Process

1

Discovery

We map your site around what families and case managers actually search for — your capacity, specialties (memory care, respite, skilled nursing referrals), licensing, and brand details.

2

Figma UI Mockup

We design custom visual mockups showing your rooms, staff, and care philosophy, presenting them for review in 3–5 days.

3

Responsive Coded Build

We code the approved layout, optimizing for speed, local SEO, and mobile — most families search on their phone, often from a hospital waiting room.

4

Launch & Support

Connect domain names, activate SSL, set up Analytics, and provide 30-day post-launch support.

TRACK 2

Performance Paid Ad Campaigns

1

Local Market Audit

Analyze competing facilities in your service area — their pricing signals, review counts, and how they show up when a family searches "assisted living near me."

2

Profile & Ad Build

Fully optimize your Google Business Profile and build out your Google Local Services Ads listing, so you're eligible for the Google Verified badge families and case managers trust.

3

Verified Launch

Once your license and background check clear Google's verification (typically 7–14 days), your ads go live — you only pay when a real family actually calls or messages, not per click.

4

Weekly Optimization

Monitor cost per lead, cut underperforming ad copy, and report results in plain English every week.

TRACK 3

AI Assistants & CRM Workflows

1

CRM & Referral Setup

Connect your CRM and load your current inquiries, waitlist, and referral contacts — discharge planners, geriatric care managers, case managers.

2

AI Prompt Training

Configure your AI voice/chat assistant to answer the questions families actually ask first — availability, pricing range, tour scheduling — matched to your facility specifically.

3

Missed-Call Recovery

A missed call after hours often just means the family calls the next facility on the list. We text them back and capture the inquiry within 60 seconds, every time.

4

Automated Review Requests

After every tour or move-in, the CRM automatically sends a review request — keeping your Google rating current without anyone on your team having to remember to ask.

TRACK 4

Digital Presence & Referral Network Outreach

*Steps 1–2 come first — your presence has to be solid before we put you in front of referral sources, since the first thing a case manager or family does is look you up.*

1

Full Digital Build-Out

Beyond the website: we build and optimize your Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn — plus TikTok if you want it — so anyone checking you out sees a consistent, professional presence everywhere they look, not just on one page.

2

Ongoing Content & Posting

We post regularly across every platform so your presence stays active and current, instead of going stale after a one-time setup.

3

LinkedIn Referral Outreach

We build out your LinkedIn profile specifically for referral relationships, then directly connect with hospital discharge planners, case managers, and senior care nurses in your service area, introducing your facility to them.

4

Referral Relationship Nurture

We keep those contacts updated on your current availability on a recurring basis, so when they have a family to place, you're already the name they know and trust — not one they're meeting for the first time.
