Within 90 days of launch, Desert Sun Chiropractic booked 114 new patients through exclusive local lead capture and automated AI follow-up — with no additional staff needed to manage it.

The Challenge Before Craftsflow

When Dr. James Martinez first reached out, his clinic was struggling with a specific and common problem: shared leads. He was paying premium rates to national medical directory portals — yet every inquiry was simultaneously sent to 3–5 competing clinics in the Phoenix metro area.

The result? A brutal race to the bottom on price, and front-desk staff spending hours chasing cold inquiries that were already lost to competitors. After-hours web submissions sat ignored until 9am the next morning — killing conversion on the highest-intent moments.

  • ✗ Shared directory leads sent to 3–5 competitors simultaneously
  • ✗ Zero after-hours follow-up — web forms sat until 9am next day
  • ✗ Staff time wasted chasing cold, already-converted inquiries
  • ✗ No local brand identity — no Google Map Pack presence in target zip codes

The Craftsflow 3-Tier Growth System

1
Stage 1 — Week 1–2
Exclusive Local Lead Capture
We launched hyper-targeted Facebook and Google ads to zip codes within a 10-mile radius of the clinic. Ads promoted a low-friction "Free Initial Assessment" offer. Every lead came exclusively to Desert Sun — no competitors received the same inquiry.
2
Stage 2 — Week 2 onwards
60-Second Missed-Call Recovery
Every new inquiry — whether from a web form fill, phone call, missed call, or social ad click — triggered an automated SMS response within 60 seconds. The message qualified their interest, asked about their schedule, and moved them toward booking without any staff involvement.
3
Stage 3 — Ongoing
AI-Powered Calendar Booking
Hot responders were seamlessly handed off to our conversational AI appointment system. It handled scheduling, confirmations, and 24-hour reminder messages — without a receptionist touching the process. Calendar slots filled automatically, even at 2am.
